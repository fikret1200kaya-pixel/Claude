# Channel Brief — "Boardroom Tales" (working name)

## Concept
Faceless English-language YouTube channel telling business stories: how giants collapsed, how simple ideas built empires.
Tone: cinematic, clear, calm-but-gripping narrator. No financial advice, only storytelling and analysis.

## Name candidates (check availability on YouTube before registering)
1. Boardroom Tales  (working name)
2. Fallen Giants
3. The Money Narrative
4. Rise & Ruin
5. Corporate Autopsy

## Positioning
- Audience: 18-44, English-speaking (US/UK/CA/AU), interested in business, investing, career, entrepreneurship.
- Promise: "One company, one decision that changed everything, in 8 minutes."
- Revenue: AdSense (high-RPM finance/business niche), later sponsorships, affiliate (books, tools), newsletter.

## Video format (every episode)
- Length: 7-9 min (mid-roll ads possible above 8 min).
- Structure: Hook (0-30s) → Setup → Turning point → Collapse/Win → Lesson → Soft CTA.
- Visuals: stock footage, archive-style graphics, animated charts, timelines, text callouts (all licensed / original).
- Voice: single narrator (ElevenLabs), consistent every video.
- Thumbnail: 3-4 words max, one strong image, high contrast, two-color palette (deep navy + signal orange).

## Brand palette
- Navy #0B1B3A, Orange #FF6B1A, Off-white #F5F1E8, Charcoal #1A1A1A.
- Fonts: Bebas Neue (titles), Inter (body).

## Launch batch (5 videos)
1. Blockbuster — The $50 Million Mistake
2. Kodak — Invented the Digital Camera, Then Went Bankrupt
3. Nokia — From 40% of the World to Zero
4. Costco — The Store That Doesn't Want to Make Money on Products
5. WeWork — The $47 Billion Illusion

## Upload plan
- 2-3 videos per week after launch; publish all 5 in the first 2 weeks.
- Best slots to test: Tue/Thu/Sat, 14:00-17:00 US Eastern.

## Policy safety (YouTube monetization)
- Every video must carry original research, original script and editing, not template spam.
- Disclose use of synthetic voice/AI visuals where realistic (YouTube altered/synthetic content toggle when it applies).
- Use only licensed or original footage. No copyrighted clips without license/fair-use analysis.
- Frame content as information/entertainment, never personalized investment advice. Add disclaimer in description.
- Fact-check every number against primary sources before upload (see "Fact-check" notes in each script).
