## Global edit rules
- 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps. Color grade: cool navy shadows, orange highlights. Film grain 5-8%.
- Music: calm, tense underscore (royalty-free: YouTube Audio Library). Duck music -18 dB under narration.
- Cut every 4-7 seconds. Slow push-in (Ken Burns 105%) on every still.
- Text style: Bebas Neue, white, one orange keyword. Lower-third for dates (e.g. "2000").
- Footage: only licensed/original (Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons CC, own motion graphics). Avoid real brand logos/trademarked posters in frame beyond short editorial/news use.
